Interactive Cut

A fluid, slide-to-cut tool. Click and drag left/right on a curve to trim its length dynamically.



Cut Brush

Tool Settings

  • Sensitivity: How fast the cut indicator slides along the curve relative to your mouse movement.
  • Reversed Direction: By default, the tool cuts the tip of the curve off. Enable this to cut from the root direction.

Dynamic Tip Trimming
