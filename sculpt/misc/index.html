Misc Utilities: Advanced Shaping & Workflows

The Misc Utilities section contains some of GroomKit's most powerful and workflow-accelerating features. This section bridges the gap between manual sculpting and procedural generation, offering tools for clumping maps, advanced deformations, and universal curve extensions.

Using Base Mesh as target for Snapping workflow.


Standard Shaping Tools

Target Guides Tool

Quickly access XGen / Utilities / Target Tool, useful in creating clumping guides.

  • Set Target: Makes the selected guide a target.
  • Move Guides: Move selecting guides toward the targeted guide.
  • Clean Up: Removes target sphere from the scene.

Curve/Guide Extrude

Extends hair naturally by calculating the path and adding a new control point at the tip, preserving the shape.

  • Universal: Works with both Maya NURBS Curves and XGen Guides.
  • Fine control: Provides exact control for shaping hair buns or curls.


Customize Your Hotkeys

To optimize your grooming experience, almost all operations in the GroomKit menu can be assigned to custom hotkeys for a faster, uninterrupted workflow.

Automated Workflows

Speed up your blockout phase with faster guide placement and real-time visual feedback.

Conform/Snap Workflow

Shape your silhouette quickly by snapping guides directly to a reference mesh—no need to worry about the underlying blockout topology.

  • Conform/Snap Button: Snap your selected guides down onto the surface.
  • Magnet Icon: Automatically use your Base Mesh as the snap target.
  • Set Target: Pick any custom mesh in your scene to snap guides to.

Live Mirror

By dynamically reflecting mirrored guide positions across the X-axis, this feature provides a better grooming experience with instant feedback

  • Mirror Button: Generate a static, one-time mirrored copy.
  • Live Mirror: Mirror your guide edits instantly in real-time.
  • Toggle Mirror: Hide mirrored guides and show simple gray proxies.
  • Check Integrity: Scan and fix broken elements to maintain stability.
  • Bake Position: Lock in final positions and clear out setup nodes.
  • Discard: Delete the mirror setup and clean the node history.


🔧 Advanced Shaping Tools

Include toolset to manipulate both curves and guides in changing macro and micro CV control points

Applying 3x3 lattice deformer.


Lattice Deformer

Easily adjust the overall volume of your groom without losing your detailed sculpt work. This tool works seamlessly across XGen guides, Maya curves, and procedurally generated braids.

  1. Select a group of guides or curves.
  2. Division: Set the deformer resolution using the slider (e.g., 3x3x3).
  3. Shaping: Click Lattice and move the control points to shape selecting curves.
  4. Applying: Click Lattice again to apply the result.
  5. (Optional): Right-click the button for quick access to Accept, Cancel, and Preference options.

Smooth & Relax

Perfect for cleaning up jittery mouse strokes and softening the flow of your hair.

  • Smooth: Removes sudden bumps and noise without shrinking the overall length of the hair.
  • Relax: Creates beautiful, natural arcs. Higher values will gently pull a squiggly curve into a clean, sweeping C shape.
