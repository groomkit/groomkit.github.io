Hotkey Customization

Although GroomKit is feature-rich with many shortcuts and functionalities, diving between tabs and menus can become unintuitive and time-consuming.

With the new Customization button in the GroomKit Hotkeys tab, you can select only the functionalities needed for your current task and freely swap buttons in and out. This feature is designed to provide a flexible, distraction-free workspace.

Auto-registered commands in the Hotkey Editor.


Editing Mode

  • Layout: Number of columns.
  • Open: Auto-opens the hotkeys tray.
Hotkey Categories
  • Sculpting: Corresponding hotkeys in the Sculpt tab.
  • Utilities: Corresponding hotkeys in the Utils tab.
  • Profiles: Automated hotkeys for the Profiles window.
