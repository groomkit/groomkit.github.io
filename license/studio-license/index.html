Studio License

GroomKit is a proprietary commercial software designed, developed, and fully managed by IzuLab Studio. Available securely via our official Gumroad store, we provide flexible, robust licensing options specifically designed to scale effortlessly from individual freelance artists to large-scale, multi-disciplinary studio productions.

While our standard commercial licenses cover the day-to-day needs of most independent artists and small teams, we deeply understand that mid-to-large studios often require dedicated, hands-on assistance, centralized volume licensing, and highly reliable, round-the-clock support for production-critical pipelines. When deadlines are tight, you need a partner you can trust.

For studios requiring guaranteed support SLAs (Service Level Agreements) and custom pipeline integration, IzuLab Studio offers the following comprehensive commercial arrangements:

Dedicated Support Options

  • Volume & Site Licensing: For studios requiring multiple seats, we offer heavily tailored volume licensing packages and studio-wide site licenses. This approach simplifies mass deployment, streamlines software management across your entire internal and remote team, and centralizes billing.

  • Prioritized Issue Resolution: Studios operating under priority support retainers ensure that their specific bug reports, feature requests, and pipeline-blocking issues bypass the standard queue. These items are immediately moved to the top of our internal development schedule for rapid hotfixes and direct, immediate deployment.

  • Pipeline Consulting & Integration: Integrating new software can be complex. If your studio requires expert assistance integrating GroomKit into proprietary in-house tools, establishing custom export pipelines (e.g., highly specific Unreal Engine configurations, proprietary DCC interop), or linking with automated build systems, our specialized technical team is available for consulting on an hourly or structured project-contract basis.

  • Custom Feature Development: Every production has unique challenges. If your project requires hyper-specific features, custom node behaviors, specialized data handling, or tailored UX/UI adjustments not present in the standard GroomKit release, these can be commissioned directly. Our development team will build these tools to fit seamlessly and exclusively into your proprietary pipeline.

Contacting IzuLab Studio

To inquire about establishing a studio volume licensing agreement, setting up priority support retainers, discussing consulting rates, or commissioning custom development, please reach out to our enterprise coordination team directly:

Email: contact@izulab.studio

(When emailing, please include your studio name, the estimated number of seats required, the target software ecosystem, and a brief overview of your current pipeline requirements to help us expedite your inquiry).
