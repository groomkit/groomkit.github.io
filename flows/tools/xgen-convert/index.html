XGen Convert

Safely bake XGen interpolated primitives into standard NURBS curves with targeting capabilities.

Click XGen to Curves to convert primitives based on your active Preview percentage, or check Apply changes to selected curves to inject procedurally generated XGen shapes directly back into your existing hair cards.
